# Formula 1: From Sport to Business

![Report overview](images/01-big-picture.png)

---

## The questions this report answers

- Which teams actually dominated F1?
- What did Liberty Media and *Drive to Survive* really do to Formula 1's revenue, and to its audience?
- Does a bigger budget buy a championship?
- What does a full career look like season by season — the title years, the lean ones, and how often the car simply failed to finish?

---

## Pages

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The Big Picture** | KPIs across 75 seasons, a world map of every circuit ever raced, calendar growth from 7 races (1950) to 24 (2024), all-time constructors' titles |
| **Follow the Money** | F1 revenue structure 2010–2026, the Liberty Media takeover (2017) and *Drive to Survive* (2019) as reference lines, US vs global audience |
| **Eras of Dominance** | Championship-position heatmap by team and season, ribbon chart of points share, win share per season, seasons where the drivers' champion did not drive for the constructors' champion |
| **Driver Profile** | Drill-through page: career KPIs, championship-position arc, positions gained vs grid, retirement causes |
| **Money vs Podium** | Operating budget vs points, bubble-sized by sponsorship, animated by season; budget dispersion before and after the cost cap; points per $m efficiency ranking |
